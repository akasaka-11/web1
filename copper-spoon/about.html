Our Story

The Copper Spoon opened its doors as a small ten-table restaurant, run by a family who believed food tastes better when it isn't overcomplicated. Years later, the tables have grown to twenty, but the kitchen still runs on the same idea: simple recipes, done properly, served by people who actually enjoy the work.

What We Care About

  • Fresh ingredients over complicated techniques
  • Recipes passed down and slowly improved, not reinvented every season
  • A dining room that feels relaxed, not formal
