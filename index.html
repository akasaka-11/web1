Welcome

The Copper Spoon started as a small family kitchen and grew into a neighborhood restaurant where people come back for the same dish they had the first time. We keep the menu short, the ingredients fresh, and the service friendly. Whether you're stopping in for lunch or planning a dinner with friends, we want the table to feel like home.

Take a look at our menu, get to know the team behind the kitchen, or reserve a table for your next visit.

Why People Come Back

  • Fresh, local ingredients — sourced from nearby markets every morning.
  • A menu that doesn't change every week — so you can order your favorite again.
  • A team that knows your name — after the second visit, at least.
